# Platform Overview

The platform engineering team delivers self-service capabilities for application teams: golden-path scaffolding, environment provisioning, observability standards, and governed change workflows.

## Principles

- **Golden paths over raw infrastructure** — teams scaffold services from approved templates.
- **GitOps for production change** — all prod mutations go through reviewed PRs.
- **Draft-and-route** — automated assistants propose actions; humans approve before execution.

## Platform services

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Golden-path scaffold | Create a containerized service with standard K8s manifests |
| Developer sandbox | Budget-gated POC environments |
| Observability insight | Service health, SLOs, and alert summaries |
